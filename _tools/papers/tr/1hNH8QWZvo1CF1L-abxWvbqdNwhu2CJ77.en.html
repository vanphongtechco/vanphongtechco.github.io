Influence of the wear resistance of the working tool material on hydraulic breaker productivity

V. I. Bolobov, A. P. Batalov, Le Thanh Binh

International Scientific and Practical Conference "Innovations and Prospects of Development of Mining Machinery and Electrical Engineering" (IPDME-2018), Saint Petersburg, April 12–13, 2018, abstract, p. 117 · UDC 620.172.2

Abstract

During operation of hydraulic breakers, the dimensions of the blunting area of the pick tip increase owing to impact-abrasive wear. This leads to an increase in the contact area of the tool with the rock on impact, with a reduction in the intensity of impact fracture. As a result, from a certain moment onward the process of tool penetration into the rock practically ceases, and to resume it the pick has to be replaced or resharpened. Given that these operations are very labour-intensive, the selection of wear-resistant materials for manufacturing the working tools of hydraulic breakers and the development of methods that increase their resistance to impact-abrasive wear and, consequently, the service time of picks before resharpening are an urgent task.

Using the JCB HM380 hydraulic breaker (impact energy A0 = 981 J) as an example, the influence of the wear rate of its working tool (a cylindrical pick with ends in the form of a flat face and a conical tip with a spherical blunting of radius Rп that changes as the pick tip wears; made of two steels used for such purposes and differing in hardness, 38ХМ and Х12МФАх after cold treatment) on the productivity of the machine when breaking a rock massif (σсж = 50 MPa) was analysed.

Using the developed mathematical model of impact rock fracture, the penetration depth of the pick into the rock per impact αвнi and the total penetration Σαвн over a cycle of impacts (Fig. 1) were calculated and compared with the results of cinephotographic measurements of pick penetration into concrete during operation of the JCB HM380 hydraulic breaker (Fig. 2). Satisfactory agreement between the calculated and experimental results was noted.

It is shown that the use of the wear-resistant steel Х12МФАх as the pick material can increase by a factor of 2.8 (from n*1 = 29272 to n*2 = 80844) the number of impacts delivered by the pick until it is completely blunted, and the total depth of penetration into the rock (from 12.5 m to 34.6 m), compared with a pick made of a less wear-resistant material, 38ХМ steel, thereby substantially increasing the productivity of the hydraulic breaker and extending the service life of its working tool before replacement or resharpening.
