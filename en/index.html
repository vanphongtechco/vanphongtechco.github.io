About us

Van Phong Technical and Trading Company Limited was established on 25 June 2019 in Hanoi and operates in the following fields:

  • Scientific and technological research.
  • Construction consulting.
  • Testing – Inspection.
  • Design – Fabrication – Repair of equipment.
  • Trading, import – export.

Products: Materials, equipment and software for industry, healthcare, education, laboratories, computers and IT.

Main business line: wholesale of machinery, equipment and spare parts (VSIC 4659). Our multidisciplinary scope lets us support customers from concept and design to supply, installation and maintenance.

  • Mission: Reliable engineering solutions for every customer.
  • Vision: A leading engineering partner in industry, healthcare and laboratories.
  • Core values: Accuracy – Dedication – Efficiency.
