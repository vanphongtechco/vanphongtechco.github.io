    CRS1414 intermeshing rotary disc screen in operation at Lam Dong Aluminum Company – TKV

    The CRS1414 intermeshing rotary disc screen (150 t/h, 14 shafts, 1400 mm screen width, undersize ≤ 8 mm) was installed in the coal screening and crushing system of the Thermal Power Workshop at Lam Dong Aluminum Company – TKV, overcoming the adhesion and blinding that affect vibrating screens with high-moisture coal.

      Performance evaluation of the CRS1414 intermeshing rotary disc screen in the coal preparation system for a circulating fluidized bed boiler at Lam Dong Aluminum Company

      Đánh giá kết quả ứng dụng sàng đĩa quay đan xen CRS1414 trong hệ thống chuẩn bị than cho lò hơi tầng sôi tuần hoàn tại Công ty Nhôm Lâm Đồng – TKV

      Le Thanh Binh

      Scientific paper, 2026

      Abstract

      The paper presents the operating principle, structural characteristics and application results of the CRS1414 intermeshing rotary disc screen at the Thermal Power Workshop of Lam Dong Aluminum Company, introduced to overcome material adhesion and blinding of vibrating screens when screening high-moisture coal. Test results showed an average screening efficiency of 85.8% at an average coal moisture of about 8.2%; after cleaning the system, improving feed distribution and lining the feed chute with composite material, the efficiency rose again to above 90%. The proportion of particles below 1 mm in the processed coal decreased from 53.1% to 42.1%, limiting over-crushing, while particles larger than 8 mm increased from 2.2% to 5.5%, mainly elongated, flat or flaky particles, with no particles larger than 16 mm detected.

      Modeling impact fracture of rock by hydraulic hammer pick with regard to its bluntness

      V.I. Bolobov, S.A. Chupin, Le Thanh Binh

      Eurasian Mining, 2022, No. 1, pp. 72–75 · DOI: 10.17580/em.2022.01.15

      Abstract

      A mathematical model of impact penetration of a hydraulic breaker pick (JCB HM380) into rock is developed on the basis of Prof. V.B. Sokolinsky's model, taking into account the degree of pick bluntness under single and multiple impacts. The calculated penetration depth was compared with experimental data obtained by breaking concrete with the JCB HM380 breaker, with penetration measured by frame-by-frame video analysis. Pick blunting causes a monotonic increase in rock resistance to penetration, while penetration depth and blow duration decrease (by 28% and 20%, respectively, relative to the initial state of the tool).

      Abstract

      The wear rate of 38KhM, U8 and Kh12M steels commonly used for hydraulic breaker picks was determined with an additional cold-treatment step included in their heat treatment.     7. 2019

      Crack propagation in rock upon impact

      О распространении трещины по горной породе при ударе

      V.I. Bolobov, Le Thanh Binh, V.A. Plashchinsky

      Obogashchenie Rud (Mineral Processing Journal), 2019, No. 6, pp. 3–8 · Scopus Q2, VAK · DOI: 10.17580/or.2019.06.01

      Abstract

      Assuming that a rock fragment fractures on impact when the emerging crack reaches one of its free surfaces, the crack propagation velocity was estimated experimentally and by calculation on Vyborg granite samples. The most effective impact distance from the fragment edge (giving the largest broken-off piece) and its dependence on the diameter of the impactor's blunt face were established, and single-impact parameters (rock resistance force, contact stress, penetration, impact duration) were calculated. The crack velocity is close to 11 m/s regardless of blunt-face size and impact duration, considerably lower than in other studies, which is explained by differences in the fractured medium and impact velocity.

      Regularities of Material Destruction of the Impactor in Repeated Single Punch

      Закономерности разрушения материала ударника при повторяющихся единичных ударах

      V.I. Bolobov, Le Thanh Binh

      Journal of Mining Institute (Zapiski Gornogo Instituta), 2018, Vol. 233, pp. 525–533 · Scopus Q1 · DOI: 10.31897/PMI.2018.5.525

      Abstract

      The method and results of experiments on the wear of the cone-shaped tip of a freely falling impactor made of 38KhM, U8 and Kh12MF steels (standard heat treatment and additional cold treatment) under up to 10,000 single impacts on granite, in conditions close to those of a hydraulic breaker pick, are presented.       Regularities of Rock Destruction with a Single Blow

      Закономерности разрушения горной породы единичным ударом

      V.I. Bolobov, A.P. Batalov, Le Thanh Binh, G.A. Yusupov

      Gornoe oborudovanie i elektromekhanika (Mining Equipment and Electromechanics), 2017, No. 6, pp. 43–48 · VAK

      Abstract

      A method and test rig for studying pick penetration into rock and impact-abrasive wear of the pick material are described; freely falling strikers of steels 38ХМ and 55С2 impacting granite (f = 6.2) were used to study rock destruction by a single blow, taking into account blunting of the striker tip. Based on the two-element classical impact system, a mathematical model of penetration is proposed; calculations confirmed by experiment show that penetration depth decreases logarithmically with increasing blunt-area diameter. The critical blunt-area diameter d* = 2.45 mm (≈2.3 mm experimentally) is reached after 124,552 blows for steel 38ХМ and after a 15 times greater number (1,849,580) for the harder steel 55С2. Pick service life and machine productivity can be increased by using more wear-resistant steels or hardness-increasing methods such as cold treatment.

      Обработка холодом как способ повышения износостойкости материала бурового инструмента

      Mining Informational and Analytical Bulletin (GIAB), 2016, No. 11, pp. 108–114 · VAK

      Analysis of the effect of cold treatment on the mechanical properties of metallic materials

      Анализ влияния обработки холодом на механические свойства металлических материалов

      Le Thanh Binh

      Innovations in Transport and Mechanical Engineering: Proceedings of the 3rd International Scientific and Practical Conference, Vol. II (Mining Machines section), Saint Petersburg, April 14–15, 2015, pp. 126–129

      Abstract

      Cold treatment (down to -80°C) and cryogenic treatment (down to the liquid-nitrogen temperature of -196°C) are widely used after quenching to improve the mechanical properties of steels, but no data on their use for mining tools had been found. This review analyses published data on the effect of these treatments on steel properties; according to these data, wear resistance can increase up to 3-fold after cold treatment and up to 8-fold after cryogenic treatment. Both treatments are concluded to be applicable as additional heat-treatment steps to increase the wear resistance of medium- and high-carbon steels used for hydraulic hammer tools and other mining equipment.

      Abstract

      The utility model relates to the mining industry, namely to rotary cutters for breaking mineral and artificial materials of a wide range of strength and abrasiveness, used in driving mine workings or in mineral extraction with shearers or roadheaders. The cutter comprises a body with a wearing head part reinforced with a cemented carbide insert and a shank, with a core of high-impact-toughness material; the facing of the head part is made as a solid shell of high-strength, highly wear-resistant material (e.g. tool steel) joined to the core by diffusion welding. This increases the hardness and wear resistance of the head part in contact with the rock and thus the cutter's service life.
